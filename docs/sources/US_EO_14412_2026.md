This memorandum does NOT apply to National Security Systems (Sec. 4(b) preamble
excludes them from the HVA/high-impact inventory review).

## HVA / high-impact system deadlines (Sec. 4(b))

Exact sentences, extracted from the retrieved HTML:

- "OMB shall ... issue guidance requiring each agency to: (i) review their
  inventory of HVAs and high impact systems, excluding National Security
  Systems; (ii) transition all HVAs and high impact systems to use PQC for
  key establishment by December 31, 2030; (iii) transition all HVAs and high
  impact systems to use PQC for digital signatures by December 31, 2031; and
  (iv) develop and submit to the Director of OMB and the National Cyber
  Director a plan to accomplish this directive."

So, isolated:

- Key establishment: "transition all HVAs and high impact systems to use PQC
  for key establishment by December 31, 2030" (Sec. 4(b)(ii))
- Digital signatures: "transition all HVAs and high impact systems to use PQC
  for digital signatures by December 31, 2031" (Sec. 4(b)(iii))
