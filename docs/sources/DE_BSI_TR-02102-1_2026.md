## Exact wording (Section 2.1, "Use of Quantum-Safe Mechanisms")

Key agreement / encryption (classical-only, page 28 of the PDF, numbered p.28 in the
document body):

> "The sole use of classic key agreement mechanisms is only recommended until the end
> of 2031, see also Section 2.3. For applications with very high protection
> requirements, the transition to quantum-safe mechanisms should already take place by
> the end of 2030. This is according to a joint recommendation by the BSI and European
> partner authorities [29]."

Signatures (classical-only):

> "In future versions, signature methods that are not quantum-safe will therefore only
> be recommended for hybrid use. According to the roadmap proposed by the EU [1], the
> transition should take place by 2035 at the latest. This technical guideline follows
> this recommendation. Based on current knowledge, the use of classic signature
> mechanisms is therefore only recommended until the end of 2035."

Hybrid recommendation (Section 2.2, "Key Derivation and Hybridisation"):

> "To ensure the long-term security of a key agreement, this Technical Guideline
> therefore recommends the use of a hybrid key agreement mechanism that combines a
> quantum-safe and a classical mechanism."

Section 2.4 ("Quantum-Safe Key Agreement") recommends three quantum-safe KEMs for use
in that hybrid combination: FrodoKEM (Section 2.4.1), Classic McEliece (Section 2.4.2),
and ML-KEM (Section 2.4.3) -- matching the FrodoKEM/Classic McEliece/ML-KEM set named
in the task brief.

Section 2.1's classical-mechanisms-only chapter (Section 2.3) restates the 2031 cutoff:

> "Therefore, the sole use of the mechanisms in this section is only recommended until
> the end of 2031. The use of the methods in this chapter beyond 2031 is only
> recommended in combination with a quantum-safe KEM method from Chapter 2.4 and a key
> derivation from Chapter 2.2."

## Correction to the task brief

The task brief said "classical-only key agreement recommended only until 31 Dec 2031" --
confirmed exactly by the source above. It also asked to "find the exact section" -- that
is Section 2.1 (narrative recommendation) and Section 2.3 (restated for the specific
classical mechanisms chapter), both on PDF page ~28.
