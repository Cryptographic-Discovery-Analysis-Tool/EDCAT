# NIST PQC Standardization Process: HQC Announced as a 4th Round Selection

- **Publisher:** NIST
- **Date:** 11 March 2025
- **URL:** https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2025/03/nist-pqc-standardization-process-hqc-announced-4th-round-selection
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-23
- **Licence:** US Government work (public domain)

Exact sentences:

- "After a thorough evaluation in the Fourth Round of the NIST Post-Quantum
  Cryptography (PQC) Standardization Process, NIST has selected HQC for
  standardization."
- "HQC will be the second PQC key-encapsulation mechanism (KEM), following ML-KEM."
- "NIST will draft a standard for HQC and release it for public comment. Following
  the review and adjudication process, the final version will be published in
  approximately two years."
